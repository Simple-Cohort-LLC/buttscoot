Buttscoot GP — Privacy Policy

Simple Cohort LLC · Effective October 26, 2026

The short version: you can play everything as a guest, and then we store nothing about you. If you choose to create a passkey account, we store only your game profile so it can follow you between devices. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold or shared.

Playing as a guest

No account is required. Guest progress (wallet, settings) stays on your device and never reaches our servers.

If you create an account

Accounts use passkeys — we never see or store an email address or password. An account stores exactly:

  • a random account identifier and your chosen display name
  • your character customization (colors, hair, trash-talk flavor)
  • game progression: mat points, belt, race history, and Scoot Bucks

This data exists so your grappler works across the app and buttscoot.org. It is used for nothing else: no advertising, no profiling, no sale or sharing with third parties.

Purchases

The optional in-app purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We receive a transaction identifier to credit your purchase and prevent double-crediting — never your payment details.

AI features

In-game trash talk is generated on your device when it supports it; otherwise a short, non-identifying game prompt (character names and your chosen flavor) is processed by our server and discarded.

Deleting your data

Ask and the account is gone: contact us via the App Store support link for Buttscoot GP (or this site) and we will delete your account and everything attached to it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
